## General Towing Regulations Applicable to Limpopo

Towing in Limpopo, South Africa is regulated under the National Road Traffic Act and its associated regulations, which apply uniformly across all provinces. **Trailer weight restrictions depend on the towing vehicle's capacity.** For unbraked trailers (under 750 kg), the towing vehicle's tare (empty weight) must be at least double the trailer's gross vehicle mass (GVM). For braked trailers exceeding 750 kg, the trailer weight cannot exceed the towing vehicle's tare mass or 3,500 kilograms, whichever is lower. A standard car license allows towing of trailers up to 750 kg, while heavier trailers require specific licensing.

## Technical and Safety Requirements

All trailers with a GVM of 750 kg or more must be equipped with independent braking systems that are operated by the driver of the towing vehicle. The combined length of the towing vehicle and trailers must not exceed 22 meters. Caravans and towed vehicles can be required to undergo roadworthiness testing by local authorities or licensed private testing stations. Additionally, there is an 80 km/h speed limit for vehicles or combinations exceeding nine tons in mass. Importantly, tows conveying persons are limited to 30 km/h unless the towed vehicle is a semi-trailer.

## Operator Compliance

Towing operators in South Africa, including those in Limpopo, must comply with the National Road Traffic Act, the Consumer Protection Act, and any applicable municipal bylaws. Tow truck drivers and operators affiliated with industry organizations like UTASA (United Towing Association of South Africa) must ensure proper qualifications and conduct according to established codes of conduct and legal requirements.
